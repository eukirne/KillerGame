How to play

1

Drag anywhere on the screen to move. Your character auto-attacks whatever is closest.

2

Walk over glowing gems to gain XP. On level up, pick one of three random upgrades.

3

Survive the boss every 90 seconds. Stay alive for 5 minutes to unlock the next biome.

4

Die. Keep the gold. Spend it on permanent upgrades. Pick a new hero. Go again.

Desktop works too — WASD or arrow keys. Esc pauses.

Three minutes. Or three hours.

It's the same game. That's the point.
